FAQ

What is online ENS?

Online ENS is a hybrid class where you spend 2 days a week doing PE, one day of swimming and one day of another activity. The other 3 days, you will get offroll during period 4 to work on online Canvas assignments. It is always period 4, and I highly reccomend this class because it provides a lot of flexibility and most of the time the assignments only take around 1 day to complete, so you get a lot of free time during offroll. You may not be guaranteed to get this class but if you take a 2 term elective class (eg. band, yearbook, orchestra, Nexus), then you will most likely get the class.
